What we do

Full-scope electrical & controls — from preconstruction to commissioning.

Electrical Installation

Industrial and commercial electrical from greenfield through tenant fit-out. Conduit, feeders, branch circuits, devices, and lighting.

Switchgear & Distribution

Service entrance, MDPs, panelboards, transformers, and motor control centers sized, installed, and energized to spec.

Building Automation

Honeywell Niagara 4 controls for HVAC, lighting, and energy management. Programming, integration, and commissioning by certified technicians.

Preventive Maintenance

Scheduled maintenance programs that catch issues before they shut down production — switchgear PMs, infrared scans, motor surveys, panel checks.

Energy & Lighting

Lighting retrofits, controls integration, and energy-conservation projects with measurable kWh reduction.

Code, Compliance & Safety

NEC, NFPA 70E, OSHA. Arc-flash labeling, equipment grounding, and project-specific safety plans for every job.

BIM & Revit Modeling

Coordinated 3D MEP and electrical models from our in-house Southern Design Group — clash detection and fabrication support before crews mobilize.

CAD Drafting

AutoCAD shop drawings, riser diagrams, panel schedules, and one-line diagrams — drawing-ready documentation produced in-house by SDG.

Design Documentation

Red-line revisions, as-builts, RFIs, and submittal packages. Southern Design Group keeps every project's drawing set current from kickoff through closeout.

Estimating & Takeoffs

Electrical and controls bid estimating, material takeoffs, and detailed pricing analysis — vendor-current numbers for design-build and hard-bid projects.

ICE Building Automation

A Honeywell-certified partner running on Niagara 4.

Our ICE team designs, programs, and commissions building automation systems for warehouses, offices, hospitals, and distribution centers. We standardize on Honeywell Niagara 4 and TR-100 series sensors for repeatable, supportable installations.

  • Honeywell Niagara 4 — certified programmers on staff
  • TR-100 series — current sensor standard across new builds
  • Award-winning partner — recognized by Honeywell for project execution
  • In-house training — Niagara 4 training on Tridium framework

Southern Design Group is the in-house design division of Southern Electric & Controls — alongside our ICE controls division. From conceptual layouts to coordinated 3D models, SDG keeps every project drawing-ready — and turns out 3D, game, video, and web work of its own.
